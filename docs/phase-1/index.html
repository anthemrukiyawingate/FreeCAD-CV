THE IDEA BEHIND THE GEOMETRYNOTE 01 / 04

Constraints are design decisions.

An axis-aligned rectangle has four independent parameters: x, y, width, and height. Fixing the origin removes two degrees of freedom. Lock both dimensions and the profile becomes fully constrained — ready for a PartDesign Pad.

THE SKETCH EQUATION
DOF = 4 − 2 − 0 − 0

A lock fixes a parameter at its current value. The right angles are already built into this rectangle model.
